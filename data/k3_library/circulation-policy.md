# Library Circulation Policy

## Purpose

This policy provides guidance for library patrons about the use of library resources, services, and facilities.

## Circulation Regulations for Library Materials

| Material type | Can be borrowed? | Borrow/return location | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|
| General books | Yes | Self-check machines on the first and second floors, circulation desk on the first floor, or the 24/7 return station | — |
| Reference materials and journals | No | Not applicable | Library use only |
| CDs and DVDs | Yes | Circulation desk on the first floor | — |
| Course Reserve books | Yes, for two hours | Circulation desk on the first floor | One item per user at a time; short-term use only |

## Borrowing and Renewal Rules

- Items must be returned or renewed before the due time. Overdue items cannot be renewed.
- The renewal period is half of the original loan period and renewal is allowed only when no other patron has requested the item.
- Patrons must report damage to library staff before check-out or check-in.
- If a due date falls on a holiday, it is extended to the next day the library is open.
- Patrons must not hide, misplace, or remove items without completing the proper check-out procedure.
- All loans must be returned and fines must be paid at least one month before leaving VinUni.
- Exceptions to fines or loan terms require approval from VinUni senior leadership.

## Circulation Privileges

| User group | Number of items | Loan period | Renewals |
|---|---:|---|---:|
| Undergraduate students | 3 | 2 weeks | 1 time |
| Graduate students | 5 | 1 month | 1 time |
| VinUni faculty | 5 | 6 months | 1 time |
| Affiliated faculty, full-time staff, and service-contract staff | 3 | 2 weeks | 1 time |
| Visiting students | Based on library approval | Based on library approval | Based on library approval |
| Guests, interns, and alumni | Not eligible to borrow | Not applicable | Not applicable |

Faculty borrowing textbooks for teaching may request a six-month loan. These items may be recalled if requested by other users. Requests for an extension should be sent to the library.

## Requesting Books or CDs/DVDs

Patrons may request items that are currently checked out. A request will not be accepted when:

- The item is in Course Reserve.
- The item is lost, missing, or non-circulating.
- The patron's borrowing privileges are blocked.
- The patron has reached the borrowing limit.

When a requested item is ready, it is held for two days. If it is not collected within that period, the request is canceled. Only the patron who made the request may collect the item in person.

## Recalls

All borrowed items may be recalled after one week. The borrower receives an email with a new, earlier due date and must return the item by that date.

## Library Equipment

- The loan period for library equipment is one working day.
- Equipment must be returned no later than 15 minutes before the library closes.
- Equipment overdue for more than five days is considered lost and the user must pay replacement costs.
- Equipment must be returned in person to staff at the circulation desk on the first floor.
- The library may recall equipment for maintenance or other operational needs.
- Fine waivers and loan extensions require approval from university leadership.

## Fines, Damage, and Loss

Patrons may be fined for returning items late or when library materials or equipment are damaged or lost. The applicable amounts follow VinUni's Financial Regulations and Tariff.

Minor damage includes small tears or stains on fewer than ten pages, light cover damage, light water marks, or minor equipment issues. Major damage or loss includes missing or heavily damaged covers or pages, extensive writing or stains, unreadable CDs or DVDs, and broken or lost equipment.

There is no refund when an item reported as lost is found after its replacement fee has been paid. Email reminders may be sent, but not receiving a reminder does not excuse a late return. Fine waivers are considered only in serious documented circumstances. Fine appeals are handled by the library case by case.
