# Financial Regulations and Tariff — Library Fees

## Overdue Borrowing and Recall Fines

- Normal material: 10,000 VND per overdue document per day.
- Course Reserve material: 10,000 VND per overdue document per hour.
- Equipment: 10,000 VND per overdue item per day.

## Slight Damage Repair Fee

The repair fee for slightly damaged library material or equipment is 200,000 VND per document or item of equipment. An overdue fine or administrative charge may also apply under the Library Regulations. Students with unpaid fees cannot continue borrowing library materials or equipment.

## Lost or Badly Damaged Items

For a document that can be reordered, the replacement fee is the purchase price of a new copy plus a 200,000 VND administrative fee per document. For a document that is unavailable on the market, the fee is five times its original price.

For lost or badly damaged equipment, the fee is the purchase price of the same or equivalent equipment plus a 200,000 VND administrative fee per item.
