# Library Borrowing FAQ

## Where are the borrowing and returning rules?

The rules, regulations, and procedures for borrowing and returning books are published in the library policy on the VinUniversity Library website.

## Is there a fee to borrow books?

No. Library books may be borrowed without a borrowing fee.

## How can users borrow or return print books?

Users may borrow or return print books with a self-check machine on the first or second floor. The 24/7 return machine at the library's main entrance is for returning books only.

## How many books can each user group borrow?

| User group | Number of items | Loan period | Renewal |
|---|---:|---|---:|
| Undergraduate students, affiliated faculty, full-time staff, and service-contract staff | 3 | 2 weeks | 1 time |
| Graduate students | 5 | 1 month | 1 time |
| Faculty | 5 | Up to 6 months | 1 time |
| Visiting students | Determined by library leadership | Not stated | Not stated |
| Guests, interns, and alumni | 0 | Not eligible | 0 |

## What does this FAQ say about overdue fines?

This FAQ states the following amounts:

- Normal material: 20,000 VND per overdue document per day.
- Course-specific material: 20,000 VND per overdue document per hour.
- Equipment: 20,000 VND per overdue item per hour.

It also states that failure to pay on time does not remove the responsibility to return or renew items. If an item has not been returned within 30 days after its due date, the patron must pay both the overdue fine and an additional fee equivalent to the item's cover price.

These figures conflict with amounts on another VinUni library service page. The latest Financial Regulations and Tariff should be checked before treating a fine amount as authoritative.

## What should a patron do after losing or damaging an item?

Lost or damaged items should be reported to the library promptly. Patrons may be charged overdue fines and repair or replacement fees according to VinUni's Financial Regulations and Tariff.

Minor damage includes repairable damage such as markings, small tears, stains, or liquid damage on fewer than ten pages. Major damage or loss includes missing or unusable covers or pages, extensive markings, unreadable audiovisual items, severely broken equipment, or items reported as lost.

## What if an item is already damaged when borrowed?

The patron should report torn pages, missing pages, or markings to library staff immediately and provide specific details. This helps prevent the patron from being held responsible for pre-existing damage.

## Where can a user see due dates and overdue status?

Borrowing information, due dates, and overdue status are available in the My Library Account section of the VinUniversity Library website. Users sign in with their VinUni account.

## What if a returned item still appears as overdue?

The patron should first confirm that the book was returned through a self-check machine or the 24/7 return station rather than being placed directly on a shelf. The patron should then check for a confirmation email and contact library staff at the information desk if the issue remains.

## How can a borrowed print book be renewed?

The user signs in to the library website with a VinUni ID. The user may select Renew All for every borrowed book or use Loan and Renew for an individual book. The FAQ recommends renewing one day before the original due date.

## Can users share library cards?

No. Sharing a VinUni library ID or library card is prohibited.

## What if the security gate alarm sounds after self-checkout?

The borrower should bring the book to the circulation desk on the first floor for assistance.

## Can library equipment be used outside the building for a long event?

Normally, no. Library equipment may be used for one working day inside the library. Some external events may receive an exception when approved through the event sponsor and library manager.
