# Library Fines and Online Payment Guidelines

## Accepted Payment Methods

VinUni accepts Visa, MasterCard, American Express, JCB, UnionPay, and ATM cards from local banks. Library fines, fees, and refund policies are governed by VinUni's Financial Regulations and Tariff.

## Starting an Online Payment

1. Sign in to My Library Account from the library homepage with a VinUni account.
2. Select Pay Fine.
3. Select all items for full payment or selected items for partial payment.
4. Accept the terms and conditions and proceed to checkout.
5. Select a payment method and complete the payment provider's required verification.

## Card and QR Payments

Credit and debit card payments require the information and verification requested by the issuing bank. ATM-card or bank-account payments may require an OTP. For QR payment, the user selects a supported mobile banking application and scans the displayed QR code.


## After a Successful Transaction

For a full payment, the user selects Finish. For a partial payment, the system displays the remaining fees and offers the choice to continue payment or finish the current session.

An online receipt is automatically sent to the email address registered in the VinUni Library system.

## Unsuccessful Transactions

A transaction may fail because the session expired, card information was incorrect, the available balance was insufficient, online payment was not enabled, the issuing bank declined the transaction, or required card verification was not completed.

When a transaction fails after accurate information has been entered, the user should not repeatedly submit another payment. The user should contact library staff at the circulation desk for assistance.
