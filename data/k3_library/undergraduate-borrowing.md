# Undergraduate Student Borrowing Policy

## Library Card

A VinUniversity ID serves as the user's library card for borrowing library materials and equipment.

## Borrowing Privileges

Undergraduate students may borrow up to three items at a time. The loan period is two weeks per item.

## Renewal

Books may be renewed once for one additional week when both of the following conditions are met:

- The book is not overdue.
- No other patron has requested the book.

Books may be renewed online through the library website or on site with the self-check machines on the first or second floor.

## Requests and Holds

Students may request books through the VinUni Library online catalogue. Requests normally take one to two business days to prepare. The library sends an email when an item is ready for collection, and its current status is also available in the user's library account.

Requested items are held for two days. Books may be checked out at the circulation counter or with a self-check machine.

## Equipment Loans

- Equipment may be borrowed for one working day.
- Equipment is due 15 minutes before the library closes.
- Equipment overdue for more than five days is considered lost and the borrower is charged for a replacement.
- Equipment must be returned directly to library staff at the circulation desk on the first floor.
- The library may recall equipment for maintenance or other operational reasons.

Available equipment may include laptops, iPads, chargers, mice, headphones, adapters, pointers, markers and erasers, and lockers.

## Fines and Other Charges

Patrons may be charged for overdue materials or equipment and for repair or replacement of damaged or lost items. The amounts are governed by VinUni's Financial Regulations and Tariff. Appeals are considered by the library case by case.
