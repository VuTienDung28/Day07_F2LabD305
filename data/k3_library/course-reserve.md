# Course Reserve Policy

## Purpose

Course Reserve provides students with access to selected course readings in print and electronic formats, including books, lecture notes, e-books, e-journal articles, and scanned excerpts.

## Student Use

Physical Course Reserve items are kept at the circulation desk and organized by call number. Students need the call number to request an item.

Course Reserve materials may be used only inside the library for a maximum of two hours. A user may borrow no more than two reserve items at a time.

## Faculty, Staff, and Instructor Requests

Physical materials are placed in Course Reserve collections on the first floor at the request of faculty, staff, or instructors while courses are being offered.

The Course Reserve request form, available on the library website or at the circulation desk, should be submitted at least eight weeks before the course starts.
